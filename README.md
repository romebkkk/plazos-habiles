# 📅 Plazos Hábiles

> When does my legal deadline expire? Administrative business days (Law 39/2015), calendar days or court deadlines (August excluded), with Spanish national holidays and your own regional ones.

**Free · open source · 100% in your browser (nothing is uploaded).**

<sub>[🇪🇸 Español abajo](#-español)</sub>

## Features
- Easter computed algorithmically
- last-day rollover
- custom holidays

## Use
Open `index.html` — no install, no server, no build. Host it free on GitHub Pages.

## Basis
Ley 39/2015 art. 30; LOPJ arts. 182-183.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
Orientative tool — not legal, tax or financial advice. Check your specific case with a professional.
